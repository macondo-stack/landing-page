The BSGO lens

Every topic we study is framed by one of four perspectives on the lifecycle of a data or GenAI application.

B

Build

Designing agents, pipelines, and models: protocols, tools, retrieval, and the smallest version that works.

S

Scale

Running them reliably: deployment, state and memory, persistence, networking, and performance.

G

Govern

Keeping them safe and accountable: identity, delegated authorization, policies, and data protection.

O

Optimize

Making them better over time: evaluations, observability, quality gates, and cost.

Study case principles

  • Simple and composable — small enough to combine with other cases.
  • Tiered — basic, medium, and advanced, each building on the one before.
  • Self-contained — description, plan, infra, runbook, and code in one folder.
  • Runs locally — even when it reaches cloud resources.
  • No leftovers — every deploy has a teardown.
